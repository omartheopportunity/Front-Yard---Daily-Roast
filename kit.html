The Room Entry Kit.
Never freeze at "what do you do?"

Your 30-second intro, 3 openers, and the follow-ups that turn a conversation into a next step. Use it at your first New Faces New Places this Thursday.

Part 1

Your 30-second intro

Fill in the blanks out loud once before you walk in. Keep it under 30 seconds.

The formula "I help [who] go from [where they're stuck] to [what they want]. Right now I'm working on [your current project], and I'd love to meet [the type of person you want to meet]."
Example "I help 9-to-5 professionals build a network outside their job. Right now I'm launching a side business in marketing, and I'd love to meet founders who've made the jump from corporate."

The last line is the most important. It tells people exactly who to introduce you to.

Part 2

3 openers that start real conversations

Skip "so what do you do?" Pick one.

Opener 1"What are you working on right now that you're excited about?"
Opener 2"Who's one person you'd love to meet this month?" Then listen. You may know them.
Opener 3"What would make this conversation worth your time?" It sets the agenda and shows you respect their time.
Part 3

Follow-up scripts

Send within 24 hours, while they still remember you.

Same-day text or DM "Great talking to you, [name]. Loved what you said about [specific thing]. I'm going to send you [a person, link, or idea] that might help."
The give (send it two days later) "Thought of you. [Name] is doing [relevant thing] and I think you two should talk. Want me to make the intro?"
The ask (day 5 to 7) "Hey [name], I'm working on [project]. You mentioned you know [type of person]. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute intro?"
Part 4

The 7-day follow-up plan

  1. Send the same-day message to everyone you clicked with.
  2. Connect on LinkedIn or Instagram. Add a note about what you talked about.
  3. Send the give. An intro, a link, or an idea. No ask.
  4. Comment on one of their posts. Be specific.
  5. Check in. Ask how the thing they mentioned is going.
  6. Make the ask. One clear request, using the script above.
  7. Book the next conversation. Put a time on the calendar.
